2

INVESTMENT BOOTCAMP
Step 01 of 08
07

Bibliotheca

Write down what you believe about the market, and the evidence behind it.
In plain words
A literature review with a thesis at the end of it.
It produces
A referenced hypothesis, in the repo, dated.
It prevents
Backtesting a hunch you cannot defend afterwards.
Step 02 of 08
08

Universe

Decide which securities were investable, as of every date in the test.
In plain words
The eligible list, rebuilt for each date rather than today.
It produces
A point-in-time membership table with liquidity filters.
It prevents
Survivorship bias — testing on the winners that survived.
STEP 03 OF 08
09

Data

Pull, clean and check the series you will actually compute on.
In plain words
Curation, then refinery, then analysis — in that order.
It produces
A reproducible dataset written to Output/.
It prevents
Beautiful results that came from broken inputs.
Step 04 of 08
10

Portfolio

Turn a signal into weights, limits and a rebalancing rule.
In plain words
How much of what, and how often you change your mind.
It produces
A weighting scheme with position and turnover limits.
It prevents
A good signal in a portfolio nobody could hold.
Step 05 of 08
11

Backtest

Run the rules over history, with costs, without peeking ahead.
In plain words
The simulation, run by the Backtest Engine.
It produces
A performance curve and a cost-aware track record.
It prevents
Paper returns that real trading would have erased.
Step 06 of 08
12

Attribution

Split the return into known factor exposure and what is left.
In plain words
Which part of the return did you actually earn?
It produces
A factor and idiosyncratic breakdown of performance.
It prevents
Selling factor beta as if it were alpha.
Step 07 of 08
13

Paper trading

Run it forward on live data, with no money at risk.
In plain words
A dress rehearsal on data nobody has seen yet.
It produces
Out-of-sample evidence and an operations checklist.
It prevents
Finding the plumbing problems on day one of funding.
Step 08 of 08
14

Production.

The strategy leaves the repo and joins the KN Fund allocation.
In plain words
Real capital, real monitoring, a real drawdown policy.
It produces
A funded, monitored strategy with an owner.
It prevents
Research that stays research forever.

Section 03

The Investment Lab

Two ways in: the platform, or the library.
18
The platform
19

Six modules.

One module per stage of the process. Each reads the previous module’s output, so you can enter the pipeline wherever your work already is.
01
Data Curator
Pulls raw market and fundamental data from any provider and aligns it on one calendar.
This session’s demo
02
Data Refinery
Cleans, adjusts and reshapes the curated data into analysis-ready series.
03
Data Analyzer
Builds features and tests whether they carry signal, before you model anything.
04
Backtest Engine
Runs the rules over history with costs and no look-ahead, and returns the track record.
05
Portfolio Construction
Turns a signal into weights, position limits and a rebalancing rule.
06
Attribution Analysis
Splits the return into known factor exposure and the part that is actually yours.
The platform

No code required.

The same pipeline, driven from a workspace instead of a terminal: build a universe, download data, construct a portfolio, run a backtest and attribution — each step tracked to completion.

The demo · install
24

Let the assistant install it.

Paste this into Claude or Codex
I want to download market data using Yahoo Finance and KaxaNuk Data Curator, please help me install: https://kaxanuk-data-curator.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/quick_start.html
It reads the quick-start, checks your Python version, runs the install and creates the project. You answer its questions and watch the terminal.
What it will run
$ pip install --upgrade pip
$ pip install kaxanuk.data_curator
$ pip install kaxanuk.data_curator_extensions.yahoo_finance
$ kaxanuk.data_curator init
Why Yahoo Finance
No API key. Thirty laptops, zero sign-ups.
init creates
Config/ · Config/.env · Output/ · __main__.py
If the third command fails, your interpreter is 3.14 — nothing else is version-sensitive.
